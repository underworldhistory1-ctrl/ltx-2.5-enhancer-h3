LTX 2.5 Architecture & Quick Tips

Refine Details: Uses the official IC-LoRA (1.0) with distilled 8-step sampling. Ideal for high-fidelity videos that need micro-textures, crisper edges, and natural photographic grain.

Restore: Specifically tuned for heavily compressed, blurry, or low-bitrate clips. Removes blocky compression artifacts and stabilizes temporal flickering.

GPU Mutual Exclusivity: The studio continuously monitors Minimax H3 on port 8188 and external GPU usage. If H3 is rendering, LTX jobs automatically wait safely in queue without conflicting for VRAM.
